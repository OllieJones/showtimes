List your upcoming shows and other events.

== Description ==

To generate a nice list of upcoming shows (or other events) on your blog page, simply write blog posts, put them in the "show" category, and fill in the Showtime field.

You can get lists of showtimes with these shortcodes.

* `[showtime]` for future shows.
* `[showtime when="past"]` for future shows.
* `[showtime when="today"]` for today's shows.
* `[showtime when="all"]` for all shows.


== Installation ==

The usual ways.

== Frequently Asked Questions ===

= Does this plugin slow down my site? =

**No**. It simply adjusts a couple of display elements for posts in the "show" category.
